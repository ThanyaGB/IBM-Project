Real examples from our database

Common myths, corrected with care

Every answer is grounded in verified health literature — never invented statistics or generic AI output.

✗ Myth

The MMR vaccine causes autism in children.

✓ Verified Fact

The MMR vaccine does not cause autism. The 1998 claim was retracted due to fraud. Dozens of studies involving millions of children confirm there is no link.

📚 WHO Vaccine Safety Advisory Committee; CDC Vaccine Safety Datalink

✗ Myth

Antibiotics can cure the common cold and flu. You can stop taking them once you feel better.

✓ Verified Fact

Antibiotics only work on bacteria — not viruses. Stopping early encourages drug-resistant bacteria. Always complete the full course as prescribed.

📚 WHO Global Action Plan on Antimicrobial Resistance

✗ Myth

Wrapping a feverish child in heavy blankets helps "sweat out" the fever faster.

✓ Verified Fact

Heavy wrapping traps heat and can dangerously raise temperature. Keep the child lightly dressed, offer fluids, and use paracetamol at the correct dose.

📚 WHO Integrated Management of Childhood Illness (IMCI)

Built for trust

Safety and sourcing you can rely on

For a health product to be trusted by communities, every design choice must prioritise accuracy, safety, and transparency — not just convenience.

📖
WHO & Ministry of Health sourced

Every fact in the database is drawn from verified WHO guidelines, national health programme documentation, and peer-reviewed public health literature. Peer-reviewed research found a false message carrying a “source” is marked true 6× more often — so every answer shows its real, verifiable citation.

🔒
Privacy by design

Phone numbers are never stored in logs. Only a short cryptographic hash is kept for analytics — even the dashboard operators cannot reverse-identify any user.

📊
Real-time public health signal

The analytics dashboard surfaces emerging myth topics to public health officials before they spread — acting as an early-warning system for health misinformation.

📊
For public health officials

The live analytics dashboard shows query volumes, trending rumour spikes, emergency flags and the myth review queue — open it alongside the demo and watch activity arrive in real time.

🚨
Emergency safety net built in. A deterministic keyword detector (zero AI involvement) scans every message for emergency signals such as "chest pain," "not breathing," "seizure," or "unconscious." When matched, the bot immediately redirects to local emergency services and halts all AI processing — because in a real emergency, speed and certainty matter more than any chatbot response.
